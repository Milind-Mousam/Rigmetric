🎮 Built for real-world performance

Minimum requirements don't always tell you what actually matters. RigMetric aims to answer the more useful question: "What kind of experience can I realistically expect?"

📊 Estimates, not magic numbers

FPS depends on many factors including drivers, game patches, temperatures, laptop power limits, background applications, graphics settings, and more. RigMetric therefore uses performance ranges instead of pretending every PC will produce one exact number.
