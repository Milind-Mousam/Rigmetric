What is RigMetric?

RigMetric is designed to answer a simple question: "Can my PC actually run this game, and how well?" Instead of stopping at minimum requirements, RigMetric aims to provide practical FPS estimates and recommended settings.

How does it work?

RigMetric compares your selected CPU, GPU and RAM against a game's performance profile and estimates a realistic FPS range for the selected resolution.

Accuracy matters

FPS can vary depending on drivers, game updates, temperatures, power limits, background applications and graphics settings. RigMetric will clearly distinguish verified benchmark information from estimates.

Built for gamers

The goal is to make PC performance information easier to understand, especially for gamers using older, budget or integrated graphics hardware.
